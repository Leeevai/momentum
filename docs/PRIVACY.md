# Privacy Policy

*Effective 9 October 2026. Applies to Momentum for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and its
widgets.*

**Momentum collects no data.** There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and
no third-party code that reports anything anywhere. The developer never receives your goals,
history, journal or anything else you put in the app.

## Where your data lives

- **On your devices.** Your goals, sessions, journal and settings are kept in one file in the
  app's own storage on your Mac, iPhone or iPad, shared only with Momentum's widgets on that
  device. A dated copy is kept on the device for 14 days, as a backup.
- **On your Apple Watch.** The Watch app receives a summary of today from your iPhone, directly
  over Apple's watch connection, and keeps it on the watch.
- **In a folder you choose, if you turn on sync.** Momentum then writes a copy of your data to that
  folder (for example in your iCloud Drive) so your other devices can read it. That folder is
  yours, and it is stored by whichever provider you picked, under their privacy policy (for iCloud
  Drive, [Apple's](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)).
- **In files you export.** A JSON backup or CSV goes wherever you save it.

Deleting the app deletes its data on that device; a sync folder or exported file stays until you
delete it.

## The network

Momentum connects to the internet in exactly one case: when you **search for a book**, the text
you type is sent to [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org), a service of the Internet Archive,
and the covers of books you add from the results are downloaded from Open Library and kept on your
device. Open Library sees the search text, that the request comes from Momentum, and, as with any
connection, your IP address; nothing else. Links you add to a goal open in the browser or app they
point to, only when you open them.

## To-dos from videos

When you turn saved videos, screenshots or a caption into to-dos, Momentum reads them **on your
device**: it measures each video, recognizes the speech and the text on screen, and names the to-dos
with Apple's on-device language model. The videos, images and text never leave your device, and
the copies made while reading them are deleted when you close the window; any that a crash leaves
behind are deleted the next time you add videos, once they're a day old. The first time, the
system may download Apple's speech recognition model for your language; Momentum itself sends
nothing.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: reminders and focus timers are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a
  server.
- **Photos**: when you save a share card or your week as an image, Momentum asks to add it to your
  photo library. It can't read your library: to bring in videos or screenshots, you pick them in
  the system's photo picker, which hands Momentum only the items you choose.
- **Files and folders**: Momentum opens only the files and the sync folder you pick.

Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, widgets, Live Activities and Focus filters work through the system on
your device. Nothing they show is sent to the developer.

## Children

Momentum collects nothing from anyone, children included.
